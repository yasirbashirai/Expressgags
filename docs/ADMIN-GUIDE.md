# Express Bags Platform — Team Guide

A plain-language guide for the Express Bags office, dispatch, accounting and drivers.

## Signing in
- **Office team:** `www.express-bags.com/admin` → email + password (+ 6-digit code from your authenticator app if two-factor sign-in is on).
- **Drivers:** `www.express-bags.com/driver` on your phone. Tip: "Add to Home Screen" to use it like an app.
- **Customers:** `www.express-bags.com/account`.
- Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. Five wrong tries lock the account for 15 minutes.
- Turn on two-factor sign-in: Admin → **My security**.

## Who can do what
| Role | Can use |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including users, pricing approval and feature switches |
| Staff | Quotes, quotations, customers, orders, content, team & fleet |
| Dispatcher | Quote requests, orders, dispatch board, exceptions, storage, shipments, team & fleet (no money) |
| Accounting | Invoices, payments, refunds, reports, customers (no dispatch changes) |
| Driver | The driver app only, and only their own assigned jobs |

## A job from start to finish
1. **Request arrives** — website quote form, concierge desk, AI assistant or phone. It appears in **Quote requests** with an AI summary (when enabled).
2. **Price it** — open the request → **Build quotation**. Add lines (truck, driver, helper, mileage, waiting, storage, handling…). The side panel shows price, cost, profit, markup % and margin % (internal only). Set a deposit if needed → **Send**. Or use **Convert to order** for a confirmed job without a quotation.
3. **Customer accepts** — they open the link, type their name to accept and pay any deposit online. An **order** is created automatically with an ID for every bag (e.g. EB-261015-7K3P-01).
4. **Print bag tags** — order page → **Tags** (4x6 labels with QR codes) and **Manifest**.
5. **Dispatch** — **Dispatch board** → pick the date → assign a driver, helpers and a vehicle. Warnings show conflicts (time overlaps, time off, missing or expired airport/port credentials, vehicle problems). The customer is notified automatically.
6. **Driver app** — the driver accepts, goes en route, confirms the bag count, scans every bag, takes condition photos, then at delivery re-scans, gets the recipient's name and signature. Problems are reported with **Report a problem**.
7. **Customer updates** — sent automatically by email (and text/WhatsApp when enabled), in English or Spanish. Customers can track at `/track` with their reference and email, phone last 4 digits or delivery ZIP.
8. **Survey** — sent automatically after delivery. Happy customers are invited to leave a Google review; unhappy ones alert the office.
9. **Invoice & payment** — **Invoices & payments** → create an invoice from one or more orders (monthly invoices for company accounts with PO numbers) → send. Customers pay online; you can also record check/ACH/cash payments and refunds.
10. **Dashboard & reports** — today's jobs, revenue, open invoices, exceptions, and performance against the six-month targets.

## Daily checklist
- **Dashboard**: today's jobs and anything unassigned.
- **Exceptions**: the office only needs to look at problems, not every normal order. Acknowledge and resolve each one with a note.
- **Quote requests**: answer new requests quickly; response time is measured.
- **Storage**: today's check-ins and releases, overstays.

## Customers & company accounts
- **Customers & accounts**: approve business account applications, set payment terms, PO requirement, credit limit and negotiated pricing.
- Company admins invite their own colleagues; hotel concierge desks send guest luggage requests from `/account/concierge`.

## Team, fleet & credentials
- **Team**: profiles, weekly availability, license expiry, private documents, create a driver login.
- **Fleet**: vehicles, maintenance, registration/insurance/inspection dates and documents.
- **Credentials**: TWIC, airport badges, port passes, insurance certificates, DOT/MC — filter by "expiring in 30 days". Reminder emails go out automatically every morning.
- Privacy rule: team names, photos and details are never shown on the public website. A customer only sees their assigned driver's first name, and only after assignment.

## Website content
**Website content** → blog posts, testimonials (only with written permission), gallery photos (alt text required), downloads, and History & names (client names stay generic unless written permission is recorded). Published changes appear on the website within minutes.

## Settings & pricing
- All prices are set here and can change any time without a developer: local zones and per-mile rates, storage rates, carrier margins, minimum prices and profits, deposits, cancellation rules.
- **Markets & zones**: markets, zones and sub-zones (e.g. the Keys), known hotels/airports/ports with instructions.
- **Test pricing** (Shipments): run 20–30 realistic scenarios; only the owner can then approve and switch on instant online pricing.

## Rules we never break (from the company's brand and privacy guidelines)
- Public contact is only the P.O. Box and the office phone. Never the street address or personal numbers.
- No promises like "24/7", "guaranteed", "always on time" or "licensed and insured" unless documented.
- We do not offer household moving or food/catering delivery.
- Protection is never called "insurance".
